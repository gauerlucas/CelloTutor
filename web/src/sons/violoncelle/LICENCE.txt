Pack description
----------------

Three full octaves of notes in a chromatic C scale, played by a real cello (with bow). Files from this pack can be used with mixing and editing software to incorporate real cello sounds into your songs. 

Some rattles and background noise. I&#x27;m still trying to tweek settings and microphone placement to get good-quality sound recordings of my cello. Message me here or email flcellogrl@gmail.com if you like this idea and need better-quality recording samples.
